# Ringworld Scattering 1.0.3

## More efficient water rendering

- Skip the water background copy when no visible ring water needs it.
- Reuse wave setup, shader lookups and matching underwater terrain samples instead of rebuilding them repeatedly.
- Avoid inactive atmosphere-layer work while retaining above-water and underwater scattering, transparency, refraction and animated waves.

Visibility, water transparency, absorption and scattering checks passed. No rendering-distance or quality-preset reduction is applied.

## Updating and requirements

Close KSP, merge GameData at the KSP root and replace matching files. Saved settings and ring geography are preserved. Requires NivenRingworld >=1.1.8; use base 1.1.10 for the new water-visibility optimization. Older supported hosts retain the previous background-copy behaviour.

The base's Harmony/ModuleManager/config requirements remain unchanged. Clouds, Parallax and Cyla are optional; upstream Scatterer is not required. Dependencies are separate downloads. Validation covered KSP 1.12.5 Windows/D3D11 and targeted optical checks, not every desktop preset or graphics backend.
